ABOUT THE PROJECT

Lodha Elise, Worli

Rising in the heart of Worli, Lodha Elise is an exceptional luxury residential development designed to redefine urban living. Spread across a 6-acre land parcel, the project features two exclusive residential towers, with 4 acres dedicated to residential development and over 80% open space, creating a rare balance of greenery, privacy, and expansive living. Designed by SCDA, the development combines striking architecture, floor-to-ceiling glass façades, spacious private decks, and elevated sea and city views to deliver an extraordinary living experience in South Mumbai.
